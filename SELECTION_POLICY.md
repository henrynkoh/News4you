# Social Blade Selection Policy

Social Blade's public top list ranks YouTube creators, not individual uploads. News4you uses a transparent two-step proxy:

1. **Creator selection:** first visible creator in the Global / All / Top list ordered by total Views.
2. **Topic selection:** newest unprocessed public upload in that creator's official YouTube Videos > Latest list.

The ranking is observed evidence, not a claim that the selected upload is YouTube's most-viewed or most-popular video. Each run records the source URLs and Pacific observation time. A stale ranking or repeated video ID cannot be reused merely to satisfy the four-video target.
