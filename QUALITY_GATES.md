# Quality Gates A-G

## Gate A: Selection integrity
- Social Blade filters and observation time recorded.
- #1 channel and source URL verified.
- Newest unprocessed official upload selected.

## Gate B: Factual accuracy
- Every objective claim maps to an inspected source.
- Dynamic metrics are time-qualified.
- No unsupported “viral,” “breaking,” “best,” or popularity claim.

## Gate C: Copyright and transformation
- Zero source footage, audio, music, lyrics, thumbnail, or copied script/description.
- Public visuals and narration have documented rights.
- Independent news purpose and source attribution are clear.

## Gate D: Creative quality
- Clear hook, readable captions, safe zones, visual variety, and no clipping.
- Substantially different treatment from prior News4you videos.

## Gate E: Technical quality
- 1080x1920, H.264/AAC, 20-45 seconds, full decode succeeds.
- Caption timing and narration alignment pass.

## Gate F: YouTube settings
- Exact @News4youa channel.
- Public, correct audience, paid-promotion, and AI settings.
- Copyright checks show no blocking issue.

## Gate G: Post-publication
- Public page opens and playback starts.
- Title, description, attribution, source link, and settings verified.
- State updated atomically.

Zero tolerance: wrong channel, copied third-party media, deceptive affiliation, unsupported claims, missing attribution, or recording a failed upload as completed.
